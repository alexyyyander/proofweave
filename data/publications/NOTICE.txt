OpenAI mathematics publication metadata

Original manuscripts, abstracts, scope descriptions and citations: OpenAI.
Source: https://github.com/openai/math
Upstream license: Apache License 2.0, reproduced in OPENAI-LICENSE.txt.

Proofweave extracts and classifies the upstream metadata, pins source links,
and adds explicit states for replay and statement review. This adapted catalog
does not attest the mathematical claims. Raw manuscripts and proof code remain
available from the original source links.

The source revision and per-file hashes are recorded in
openai-math-provenance.json. Upstream withdrawals remain visible separately
from the current manuscript index.

Reading notes added 2026-10-15: Proofweave, with AI assistance.
The notes paraphrase selected manuscript introductions and compare source
statements and configuration. Source paths, read line ranges and SHA-256 hashes
are included in openai-math-reading-guides.json. These notes are editorial
explanations, not mathematical review or verification of the original claims.
