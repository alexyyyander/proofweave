# Community guidelines

Proofweave is a research collaboration project. Treat contributors, reviewers,
and researchers with respect. Be precise about evidence and generous about
questions; disagreement about a proof, implementation, or source is not a
reason for personal attacks.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, doxxing, sharing
someone else's private work or credentials, and presenting provisional Agent
output as a verified theorem. Maintainers may remove content or pause
participation when these boundaries are crossed.

For a safety or privacy concern, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than
opening a public issue.
